FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a website take?

Starter sites are usually live in 1–2 weeks; Professional and Premium take 3–6 weeks. You get a firm timeline before we start.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, logo if you have one, photos, and what the site should do — we sort the rest together.

Do prices include hosting and a domain?

Hosting is included. A custom .com/.org domain is included in Professional and Premium.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes — redesigns and rebuilds are common projects.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Premium includes an admin dashboard. On other tiers I handle updates during the support period.

How do payments work?

Half deposit via mobile money to start, balance on delivery.
