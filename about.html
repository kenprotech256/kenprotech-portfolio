About the Studio

A one-person studio with a studio-grade standard.

No account managers, no hand-offs, no diluted vision. You work directly with the person who designs and builds your site.

Ken · Founder

Self-taught, by obsession
not by accident.

I'm Ken — the founder, designer, and developer behind KENPRO. I didn't take the university route into this. I learned to build for the web the long way: online courses, broken code at 2am, and a refusal to ship anything that felt cheap.

It became a studio when I noticed a gap. Most businesses in Uganda either had no website, or one that quietly worked against them — slow, awkward on a phone, leading nowhere. I started KENPRO to close that gap with work that actually looks and performs like it belongs on the world stage.

Today I build refined websites and platforms for businesses, real estate firms, hotels and non-profits — each one fast, mobile-first, and built to earn trust the moment it loads.

The Process

Four steps, no surprises.

A refined, transparent path from first message to a live site you're proud of.

Talk

02

Design

03
